## §5.5 — Dose-response curve (full retract)

The B-1 dose-response curve was tested across 20 seeds × 5 LOCK_AFTER
× 5 modalities × 3 SNR conditions (1500 cells × 5 LOCK = 7500 cells
total, see Appendix C). The non-monotone signature originally reported
in [previous version v0.5.7 §5.5, single seed] **does not robustly
reproduce**: pooled across 150 measurements per LOCK_AFTER, the
quadratic concavity test fails to reject the null (p=0.19).
Pre-registered subgroup replication of 2 candidate modalities
(tactile+floor SNR and force+plus10 SNR) on N=10 NEW seeds also fails
to reach Bonferroni-corrected significance (α=0.025): tactile+floor
produces c=+2.14e-6 (sign inverted, p=0.595), force+plus10 produces
c=-1.11e-5 (direction preserved but p=0.129). We retract the original
single-seed claim and report this as a methodological lesson on
multi-seed validation discipline (see ADR-0019). The dose-response
signal, if present, is below the noise floor of this benchmark and
would require substantial methodological refinement (different metric,
finer LOCK_AFTER resolution, or N≥30 per subgroup with α=0.0033 to
control for the 15-subgroup family-wise error rate) to characterize.
