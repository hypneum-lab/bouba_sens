## Version A — N12-survive (≥1 of 2 subgroups confirms)

> Selected if at least one of {tactile-floor, force-plus10} produces
> Bonferroni-corrected p<0.025 (α=0.025 / 2 PRE-REGISTERED tests)
> with quadratic peak in [85, 115].

The B-1 dose-response curve was tested across 20 seeds × 5 LOCK_AFTER
× 5 modalities × 3 SNR conditions (1500 cells per LOCK_AFTER × 5 =
7500 cells total ; see Appendix C). At the **pooled level** (150 me7
measurements per LOCK_AFTER, all modalities × SNR collapsed), the
non-monotone signature is **not** confirmed (quadratic c=-2.1e-6,
p=0.19, peak estimate LOCK≈148.9). However, **pre-registered subgroup
analysis** identifies a modality-specific signature : in the
[tactile-floor / force-plus10] subgroup the curve exhibits a concave-
down peak at LOCK_AFTER=[XX] (Bonferroni-corrected p=[XX] across the
2 pre-registered subgroups, α=0.025). This is consistent with the
Amedi sensory-substitution literature emphasizing tactile and
proprioceptive modalities (Amedi et al. 2007 ; Reich et al. 2011).
The pooled curve is washed by the 13 other modality × SNR
combinations where no non-monotone signal emerges, which itself is a
methodologically interesting observation : an Amedi-style B-1
signature in this benchmark is **modality-specific, not modality-
agnostic**, and aggregating across modalities masks it.

## Version B — N12-tie (1 of 2 marginally significant)

> Selected if exactly one of {tactile-floor, force-plus10} produces
> 0.025<p<0.05 (Bonferroni-corrected) with peak in [85, 115], and
> the other does not survive.

The B-1 dose-response curve was tested across 20 seeds × 5 LOCK_AFTER
× 5 modalities × 3 SNR conditions. At the pooled level the non-
monotone signature is not confirmed (p=0.19). Pre-registered subgroup
analysis yields one marginally significant candidate ([tactile-floor
or force-plus10], uncorrected p=[XX], Bonferroni-corrected p=[XX] ;
just above the α=0.025 threshold for 2 pre-registered subgroups).
This warrants further investigation with N≥30 seeds dedicated to the
candidate subgroup ; the §5.5 signature should be considered
**preliminary** and is not advanced as a headline result of this
paper. We retain the result as a hypothesis-generating observation
for follow-up work and explicitly flag the winner's-curse risk
(post-hoc subgroup selection from N9-Q3+ pooled-v2 informed the
choice of which 2 subgroups to test).

## Version C — N12-loses (Retract at subgroup level too)

> Selected if neither {tactile-floor, force-plus10} survives
> Bonferroni-corrected α=0.025.

The B-1 dose-response curve was tested across 20 seeds × 5 LOCK_AFTER
× 5 modalities × 3 SNR conditions. The non-monotone signature
originally reported in v0.5.7 §5.5 (single seed=0, +0.0125 peak at
LOCK_AFTER=100) **does not robustly reproduce** : pooled across 150
me7 measurements per LOCK_AFTER, the quadratic concavity test fails
to reject the null (c=-2.1e-6, p=0.19, peak LOCK≈148.9). Pre-
registered subgroup replication of the 2 candidate modalities
(tactile-floor, force-plus10) identified by N9-Q3+ pooled-v2 also
fails to reach Bonferroni-corrected significance (α=0.025 across 2
pre-registered tests). We **retract** the original single-seed claim
and report this as a methodological lesson on multi-seed validation
discipline (see ADR-0019). The dose-response signal, if present, is
below the noise floor of this benchmark and would require
substantial methodological refinement (different metric, finer
LOCK_AFTER resolution, larger N per condition, or a different
benchmark altogether) to characterize. This is — itself — a
narrow-and-falsifiable contribution of the paper : single-seed
dose-response curves on this class of benchmark cannot be trusted
without per-seed quadratic regression and pre-registered subgroup
discipline.
